Research

Humans experience the world in three dimensions. We perceive our surroundings through vision, sound, language, and touch, and we act on the world with our bodies — intelligence is grounded in this embodied, multimodal experience, not only in what we read, hear, and write. Building machines with this kind of intelligence requires rich digital representations of how people look, move, and interact in 3D environments.

My research lies at the intersection of digital humans and humanoid intelligence, spanning computer graphics and computer vision. I build technologies that understand, reconstruct, and generate realistic humans in digital form — capturing how people look, move, and interact in dynamic environments — and turn them into the data, assets, and simulation environments needed to train embodied AI.

Key problems I work on:

  • Creating faithful, controllable, and editable digital humans from images, videos, or text
  • Building high-quality 3D assets for virtual applications and humanoid robotics
  • Developing efficient, scalable real-to-sim environments and data generators for training embodied AI

Core technical areas:

  • 3D/4D human reconstruction and generation
  • Neural rendering and relighting
  • Generative AI for image and video synthesis
  • Vision-language-action (VLA) training and post-training

Through this work, I aim to develop digital humans and intelligent agents that can better understand, interact with, and assist people in both virtual and real-world settings. :)
